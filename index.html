GoHighLevel re-examined (2026-09-09, afternoon) — "lots of doctors' offices use it"

True, and the correction is owed. Run 3 labelled GoHighLevel "disqualified as-is" because its HIPAA add-on cannot be cancelled. Re-read against HighLevel's own article (HIPAA Compliance With HighLevel, modified 2026-04-01), that is a pilot-cost problem, not a compliance one: the add-on is permanent per agency account, so a practice can pilot on a throwaway agency (Starter $97 + HIPAA $297 ≈ $400/mo) and walk away. With that fixed, GoHighLevel is the strongest SaaS option in the set and the only one whose BAA is available on every plan.

What a practice actually gets, in HighLevel's words: "encryption of ePHI, Business-Associate Agreements (BAAs), audit logging, and MFA enforcement"; a BAA "provided as part of the package" and signed in-app; AES-256 at rest; protected objects = "Contacts, Notes, Custom Fields, SMS/MMS, voice recordings, email bodies & attachments, form/survey submissions, calendars, invoices. (In short, everything the account has)"; the mobile app inherits the controls; activation 48–72 h; "Agencies on Any Plan ($97 … $497) can subscribe"; SOC 2 Type II reported Feb 2026. Agency-published deployments cover dental, med spa, chiropractic, therapy, PT, optometry, urgent care and telehealth (CareStar case study, healthcare overview, med-spa review) — all agency-sourced; HighLevel itself publishes no healthcare case study.

What still has to be read in the BAA text before signing (unchanged, and now the whole question): whether live calls on LC Phone are covered as distinct from stored recordings (the article names only "voice recordings"; Twilio underneath); whether Conversation AI / Voice AI / Content AI are inside or outside scope (the article lists only Reviews AI as compliant; the exclusion list is a screenshot, not text); that deleted contacts are unrecoverable on HIPAA accounts; that Zapier and other integrations are outside the BAA; and HighLevel's own advice to keep PHI out of SMS and email bodies regardless (agency guide, setup guide, dissenting view).

Where it still sits behind Odoo Community for WCI: no SLA/escalation primitive (hand-built from Wait + If/Else); custom objects capped at 10 per location; premium workflow executions metered; PHI hosted by HighLevel and Twilio rather than in WCI's own AWS account; CloudTalk replaced rather than integrated; ~$14–28k over three years versus ~$4–11k. Where it is ahead: a visual workflow builder Carlos can edit, bundled calling/SMS/email/calendars/forms, a BAA on any plan, and a large practitioner community.

Honest ranking now: 1. Odoo Community (self-hosted) · 2. GoHighLevel (pilot first) · 3. HubSpot Enterprise · then EspoCRM / Frappe / monday.

"Does CloudTalk not work with GoHighLevel? I thought it was open so we could build on top of it"

CloudTalk ↔ GoHighLevel: no native integration on either side. CloudTalk's integrations page lists Odoo, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive and ~60 others — not HighLevel; HighLevel's phone stack is its own LC Phone (a Twilio pass-through) or a Twilio account, with third-party VoIP still an open feature request. What exists is middleware: Zapier-class connectors (Appy Pie, Zoho Flow, Latenode — none of which sign a BAA, so unusable for PHI) or a custom CloudTalk-webhook → API build that logs calls into HighLevel one-way while call control stays in CloudTalk. Sources: phone systems that integrate with HighLevel (vendor-written, May 2026), HighLevel ideas board: third-party VoIP request, HighLevel: what is LC Phone, Zoho Flow CloudTalk–HighLevel.

"Open": HighLevel is not open source. It is a commercial SaaS with a developer surface — a public REST API (contacts, conversations, calendars, opportunities, payments), OAuth 2.0 or private-integration tokens, webhooks for 50+ events, and a marketplace for apps (HighLevel developer docs). That means you can build against it (sync, log calls, push leads) but not inside it: you cannot change how its workflow engine, storage, or phone system behave, cannot self-host it, and every PHI-bearing byte lives on HighLevel's and Twilio's infrastructure under their BAA terms. Odoo Community is the inverse: LGPL code you run in WCI's own AWS account and modify at will — which is exactly what the prototype did.

Practical consequence for WCI: choosing GoHighLevel means replacing CloudTalk with LC Phone (only worthwhile if HighLevel's BAA text names live calling), not integrating the two. Choosing Odoo keeps CloudTalk with CloudTalk's own connector — pending CloudTalk's executed BAA.

"Is this really the best CRM? Nothing else HIPAA-compliant? Nothing open source?"

TCPA constraints on automated patient SMS. Verified verbatim against consolidated eCFR text: 47 CFR 64.1200(a)(9)(iv) exempts autodialed/prerecorded healthcare calls and texts to wireless numbers only if all conditions hold — number supplied by the patient; enumerated healthcare purposes only; no marketing, solicitation, billing or debt-collection content; HIPAA compliance; ~1 minute voice or ≤160 characters text; one message per patient per day and three per week maximum; STOP opt-out honoured immediately [SQ08-27].
